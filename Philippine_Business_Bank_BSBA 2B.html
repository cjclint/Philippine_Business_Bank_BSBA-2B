Philippine Business Bank

Your Business is Our Business

Welcome to Philippine Business Bank (PBB), a leading savings bank focused on serving the needs of small and medium enterprises in the Philippines.

Philippine Business Bank has established itself as a key financial institution in the Philippines, providing comprehensive banking services to businesses and individuals alike. Our commitment to excellence and customer service has made us a trusted partner for our clients' financial needs.

Company Background

Philippine Business Bank (PBB) was incorporated on January 28, 1997, as Total Savings Bank. It commenced operations on February 12, 1997, after receiving authorization from the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) to operate as a thrift bank. Later that year, on December 16, 1997, the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) approved the change of its corporate name to Philippine Business Bank, reflecting its strategic focus on business banking.

From its inception, PBB aimed to support the Small and Medium Enterprise (SME) sector, which constitutes a significant portion of the Philippine economy. The bank's initial strategy involved establishing branches in areas with high concentrations of SMEs, such as Caloocan, Malabon, Navotas, Valenzuela, and Quezon City.

Over the years, PBB expanded its branch network to other commercial and industrial centers across the country, including cities like Davao, General Santos, Bacolod, and Cebu. In 1999, the bank was granted a quasi-banking license, making it the first thrift bank to have this privilege in the Philippines.

In January 2013, PBB announced plans to expand its nationwide network of 78 branches to 100 by year-end. The first phase of expansion focused on Luzon, especially the Metro Manila area. The bank successfully launched its Initial Public Offering (IPO) on February 19, 2013, and was listed on the Philippine Stock Exchange under the ticker symbol "PBB". These additional branches were used to reach out to the Philippines' growing SME industry, which accounted for 63 percent of PBB's total loan portfolio.

Corporate Profile

Philippine Business Bank, Inc. (PSE: PBB) is a publicly listed savings bank in the Philippines, primarily serving small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs). As of December 31, 2022, PBB has grown to become the country's second-largest savings bank by assets.

  • Head Office: 350 Rizal Avenue Extension corner 8th Avenue, Grace Park, Caloocan City, Metro Manila, 1403, Philippines
  • Founded: January 28, 1997 (as Total Savings Bank)

  • Chairman: Jeffrey S. Yao
  • President & CEO: Rolando R. Avante
  • Employees: Approximately 1,748

Ownership

PBB is part of the AMY Group, a conglomerate owned by Alfredo Yao, who also owns Zest-O Corporation (a beverage company best known for its juice products) and Macay Holdings.

Business Focus

PBB specializes in providing comprehensive financial services to SMEs, including:

  • Lending Services: Term loans, working capital credit lines, auto loans, housing loans, salary loans, and second-hand auto loans
  • Deposit Products: Savings accounts, checking accounts, and time deposits
  • Trade Finance: International trade finance services
  • Treasury and Trust Services: Investment products and trust services
  • Other Services: Cash management, advisory services, bills payment/collection, group payroll services, local payment orders, mail and telegraphic transfers, night depository box, gold sale, and safety deposit box facilities

Subsidiaries and Acquisitions

PBB has expanded its operations through strategic acquisitions:

  • Insular Savers Bank, Inc. (ISBI): In 2019, PBB completed its merger with ISBI, enhancing its consumer lending business and establishing a foothold in the microfinance market
  • Bataan Savings and Loan Bank: Acquired to expand PBB's reach in the Bataan region
  • Rural Bank of Kawit, Inc.: Acquired to strengthen PBB's presence in Cavite
  • PBB-RBFI: PBB Rural Bank of Feterlino, Inc. (acquired 2015)
  • PBB Remittance Center: Handles the bank's remittance services
  • PBB Insurance Agency: Provides insurance products to bank clients

These strategic acquisitions have allowed PBB to diversify its portfolio and enhance its services to a broader client base while maintaining its core focus on serving the SME market.

Scope of Business/Operation

Philippine Business Bank operates as a full-service thrift bank in the Philippines, primarily focusing on serving small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) while also catering to individual clients. The bank's operations are structured into three main segments:

1. Consumer Banking

PBB offers a range of financial products tailored for individual clients, including deposit accounts, loan products, foreign currency accounts, and investment options. These offerings are designed to meet the diverse financial needs of individual customers.

2. Corporate and SME Banking

As PBB's core focus, this segment provides financial services to SMEs and corporate clients, offering business loans, trade finance services, and specialized financing solutions. These services are aimed at supporting the growth and operational needs of businesses across various industries.

3. Treasury and Trust Operations

This segment manages the bank's liquidity and offers investment and fiduciary services, including trust products and treasury services that cater to clients seeking professional management of their funds and fiduciary needs.

Digital and Other Services

PBB provides additional services to enhance customer convenience, including online banking, remittance services, and various other banking solutions designed to provide comprehensive financial support to clients.

Operational Reach

As of December 31, 2021, PBB operated 165 branches across the Philippines, with a significant presence in areas with high concentrations of SMEs, such as Caloocan, Malabon, Navotas, Valenzuela, and Quezon City.

Key Committees

Philippine Business Bank has established several specialized committees to support its governance framework and ensure efficient oversight of various operational aspects:

Board-Level Committees

  • Trust Committee - Oversees the bank's trust and fiduciary operations, ensuring proper management of client assets.

These committees work collaboratively to strengthen PBB's corporate governance, enhance risk management processes, and ensure regulatory compliance across all operations.

Corporate Governance

Philippine Business Bank is committed to the highest standards of corporate governance, ensuring transparency, accountability, and integrity in all aspects of its operations.

Governance Framework

PBB's corporate governance framework is based on the following key principles:

  • Board Independence - Maintaining an appropriate balance of independent directors
  • Transparency - Clear disclosure of financial and non-financial information
  • Accountability - Well-defined roles and responsibilities at all levels of the organization
  • Fairness - Equal treatment of all stakeholders, including minority shareholders
  • Integrity - Adherence to ethical standards and code of conduct

Board Committees

The Board has established various committees to assist in its oversight functions:

Corporate Governance/Nomination Committee

This committee is responsible for the development, implementation, and review of the Bank's Corporate Governance Compliance Program. It consists of at least three (3) members of the Board of Directors, two (2) of which are independent Directors.

Key Functions:

  • Assist the Board in fulfilling its corporate governance responsibilities
  • Pre-screen and shortlist candidates for Board membership
  • Ensure the Board's effectiveness and observance of governance principles
  • Conduct annual performance evaluations of the Board and its Committees
  • Oversee compliance with the Manual of Corporate Governance and Code of Conduct

Audit Committee

This committee provides oversight of the bank's financial reporting and control, and internal and external audit functions.

Key Functions:

  • Monitor and evaluate the adequacy and effectiveness of internal control systems
  • Oversee the internal audit department and appointment of internal auditor
  • Review financial statements before submission to the Board
  • Coordinate with external auditors and review audit reports

Related Party Transactions Committee

This committee reviews all material related party transactions to ensure they are conducted at arm's length and in the best interest of the bank.

Key Functions:

  • Evaluate and identify related party transactions that require review
  • Ensure appropriate disclosure of related party transactions
  • Report to the Board regularly on the status of related party transactions
  • Ensure transactions with related parties are made on market terms

Executive Committee

This committee acts on behalf of the Board on matters that require immediate attention between Board meetings, except for specific powers reserved for the Board.

Key Functions:

  • Review and approve credit proposals within authorized limits
  • Implement strategic policies established by the Board
  • Act on matters delegated by the Board of Directors

Corporate Social Responsibility

Philippine Business Bank is committed to making a positive impact on the communities it serves through various corporate social responsibility initiatives.

In August 2010, PBB, together with other companies in the AMY Group (Zesto Corporation, ZestAir, RC Cola, Amchem, and SMI Development), participated in a tree planting activity at the La Mesa Forest and Nature Reserve.
